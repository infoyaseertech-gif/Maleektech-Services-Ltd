Who is Maleektech
Services LTD?

A dynamic and innovative Nigerian company specializing in comprehensive construction solutions, telecommunications infrastructure, project management, and general contracting services.

Company Overview

Built on technical expertise and operational excellence.

With our corporate office strategically located in Funtua, Katsina State, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for construction and infrastructure development across Nigeria.

Our multi-disciplinary approach allows us to deliver integrated solutions that meet the diverse needs of our clients — from road construction and building infrastructure to telecommunications installations and property management.

Our Commitment & Philosophy

The principles that shape how every project is planned, staffed and delivered.

Our Commitment

We are dedicated to delivering construction and infrastructure solutions that meet the highest standards of quality, safety, and efficiency. Through strategic investments in skilled personnel, modern equipment, and robust project management systems, we position ourselves as a trusted partner in Nigeria's infrastructure development landscape.

Our customer-first approach ensures that we understand and meet the unique requirements of each project — whether road construction, building infrastructure, telecommunications installation, or facility management.

Our Business Philosophy

We believe in building long-term partnerships through integrity, transparency, and consistent delivery. Every project we undertake represents our commitment to excellence and our contribution to building Nigeria's infrastructure.

Our integrated service delivery model ensures seamless project execution from design through completion and ongoing maintenance.

Core Values That Guide Us

Six commitments, upheld across every division and every site.

Safety

We uphold a positive, proactive safety culture, leading by example and instilling this value in all personnel and subcontractors.

Quality

Our goal is to be the best and to continually improve — through focused teamwork, pride in our work, and requisite quality on every project.

Transparency

We behave in a manner that demonstrates trust, honesty, courage, consistency, and responsibility in every business dealing.

Integrity

We conduct business to the highest standards of professional behavior and ethics — transparent and honest with everyone we work with.

Professionalism

We serve clients and work as a team professionally and respectfully, always advancing toward mastery in our fields.

Client Satisfaction

Flexible scheduling, quality products, efficient services and innovative solutions — delivering value to every stakeholder.

Vision & Mission

What we're working toward, and how we get there.

Our Vision

To be a leading provider of quality services in our areas of specialization, recognized for excellence and professionalism.

Our Mission

To understand the expectations of each client and provide excellent, innovative, and cost-effective solutions in line with global best practices for the benefit of all stakeholders.
