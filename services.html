Road Construction

Comprehensive road construction services designed to support infrastructural development and enhance transportation networks across Nigeria — guided by precision engineering, structural durability, and strict adherence to international standards. We apply modern construction technology to extend pavement longevity and reduce lifecycle maintenance costs, handling projects from rural feeder roads to major highway construction and rehabilitation.

Road Design & Planning

  • Complete road design and engineering services
  • Feasibility studies and site assessment
  • CAD drawings and technical specifications
  • Environmental impact assessment
  • Traffic flow analysis and optimization

Surface Dressed Roads

  • Double and triple surface dressing
  • Chip seal and slurry seal techniques
  • Cost-effective pavement preservation
  • Weather-resistant finish

Asphalt Road Construction

  • Hot mix asphalt (HMA) paving
  • Asphalt resurfacing and overlay
  • Quality control and compaction testing
  • Smooth, durable wearing course
  • Long-lasting pavement solutions

Rural Feeder Roads

  • Laterite road construction
  • Gravel road development
  • Community access road projects
  • Agricultural transport corridors
  • Cost-effective rural connectivity

Drainage & Earthworks

  • Road grading and earthworks
  • Drainage construction and management
  • Erosion control structures
  • Culvert installation
  • Storm water management systems

Road Maintenance

  • Rehabilitation of existing roads
  • Pothole repair and patching
  • Preventive maintenance programs
  • Pavement condition assessment
  • Routine and periodic maintenance
